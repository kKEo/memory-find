document granularity unless the slice says `fact`. Six fact fixtures are planted after the
documents: a knowledge-update chain (an old fact superseded 100 days ago by a new one), a curated
fact citing its evidence page, two conflicting facts at different trust levels, and one fact that
is forgotten after being written; one document is forgotten after loading.*

## What changed in ranking

- **Fact arm.** Stored facts are matched by keyword and meaning and vote for their evidence
  passage with weight 0.4 (profile `default`; derivation in `profiles show`). The semantic floor
  applies to facts too.
- **Fact granularity.** `granularity=fact` returns facts with their evidence attached; when two
  facts score within 10% of each other, the more trusted one goes first. Trust decides conflicts;
  the loser is kept (schema §7).
- **`as_of`.** Documents: the revision created by then and not yet superseded. Facts: recorded by
  then and not yet invalidated. Forgotten records: never.
- **Stopwords** dropped from keyword queries unless the query is only stopwords.

## Not measured here

Trust transitions are tested, not evaluated: `internal/kb/facts_test.go` and the server tests
assert that a tool call cannot forget, supersede or promote a `user` or `curated` record and
receives the CLI command instead. Elicitation in a real client (spike S7) is deferred to P5.
The real-model numbers for these slices are not re-run in this report; the slices are lexical by
construction and the hash embedder is the gate.
